AI search visibility

When buyers ask an AI assistant who to hire, does your name come up?

More of your buyers start their research in ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini before they ever visit a website. If the assistant recommends your competitors, you can lose the deal before a conversation starts.

I test this the way a real buyer would, then show you what it takes to close the gap.

  1. Blind testingLogged out, incognito, with no history, so results reflect what a stranger sees.
  2. Real buyer questionsPrompts written the way your customers actually ask, in the order they ask them.
  3. Competitive benchmarkWhich firms get recommended instead of you, and why the assistants trust them.
  4. A plan to close the gapSearch, content, and partner moves that make you easier to find and recommend.
